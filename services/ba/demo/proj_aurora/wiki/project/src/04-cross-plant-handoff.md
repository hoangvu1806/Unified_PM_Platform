# Aurora Cross-Plant Investigation Handoff

## Org
- Originating plant owns the deviation case header.
- Sister plant may receive a linked child case when the same lot shipped between plants.
- ACT-LAB of the originating plant remains primary for release testing.

## Progress
- Case stages: Intake, Investigate, Hold, Decide Recall, Execute, Close.
- BPMN must show XOR on severity and on recall class, and AND for dual investigation tracks.
