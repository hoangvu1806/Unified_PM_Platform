# Aurora Quarantine Hold and Segregation

## Constraint
- Held lots stay segregated; sellable stock stays separate from held lots.
- Cross-plant transfer of a held lot requires ACT-QA and ACT-PM joint note.
- Negative available quantity after hold is not allowed for the same SKU identity.

## Convention
- Hold reason text cites the deviation case id and severity label.
- Plant Alpha and Plant Beta use the same hold status vocabulary.
