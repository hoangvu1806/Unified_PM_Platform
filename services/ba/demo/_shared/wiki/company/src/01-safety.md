# Nimbus Factory — Workplace Safety Ground Rules

Company: Nimbus Factory (fantasy org for BA demos).

## Rules
- All outdoor work above 2 meters must use a safety harness.
- Incomplete kits must not leave the warehouse floor.
- Rain or high wind cancels outdoor display work until a supervisor re-opens the window.
- Agents may propose drafts; only a human (ba.lead) may approve wiki pages.

## Constraint
- Safety rules override schedule pressure.
- Do not invent thresholds that are not written here.

- ACT-WH: warehouse pick-pack
