# Nimbus Factory — Core Glossary

## Glossary
- AC: Acceptance Criterion — condition that verifies a User Story.
- PO: Product Owner — approves the Business Analysis Package.
- BR: Business Rule — settled rule with a source; agents treat as constraint.
- US: User Story — As a role I want action so that value.
- UAPDF: Unify AI Product and Design Factory (platform name).

## Convention
- Project ids are hashed: PRJ- plus hex from the feature session.
- Company wiki is shared; project wiki is private to one project_id.
